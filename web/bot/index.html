GHOST GATE · TELEGRAM

Telegram access starts with an account-bound, single-use link from the official bot.

  1. 01Open the official Ghost Gate bot in Telegram and send /join.
  2. 02Open the link it sends in the browser where your Tari wallet is available.
  3. 03Approve the wallet proof and fresh challenge. The bot rechecks the bridge receipt before admission.

A link without the bot-minted token cannot authorize membership. Do not share join links.
