Telegram verification

Reading your join link…

  1. The gate issues a fresh, single-use challenge
  2. Your wallet creates the proof output — public and permanent (approval in your wallet)
  3. Your wallet signs the gate's challenge
  4. The bridge verifies against chain state
  5. A private-group join request is authorized

Proof accepted is not the same as access authorized. The bot re-verifies every proof against the bridge before admitting anyone. In local fixture mode, results are labelled test-only and never change Telegram membership.

You'll be asked for one wallet approval to create a publicly-auditable proof output (a public minimum, never a balance), and one to sign a challenge. No seed, no keys, no exact balance — ever.
